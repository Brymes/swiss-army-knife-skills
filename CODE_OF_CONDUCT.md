# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific and constructive. This project is a security tool; strong
disagreement about a finding, a payload or a design is welcome when it is backed by
evidence and aimed at the work, not the person.

Expected:

- assume good faith and ask before assigning motive;
- keep criticism technical and reproducible;
- credit prior work and upstream sources;
- respect the authorized-target and no-secrets rules;
- make space for newcomers and non-native English speakers.

Not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, slurs or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private data, credentials or target details;
- encouraging unauthorized or destructive testing;
- spam, drive-by promotion or repeated off-topic comments;
- retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any project space.
It also applies when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may warn, temporarily ban or
permanently ban a participant. Enforcement decisions are made in the project's
interest and should be explained when practical.

Report a conduct issue privately to a maintainer through GitHub. Do not include
secrets or a third-party target's private data. A maintainer with a conflict of
interest should step aside.
